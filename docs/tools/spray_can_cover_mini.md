# Spray Can Cover Mini

**Prototype — not yet verified by physical fit testing.**

A compact 24 mm tall cover with 1 mm walls and four rounded inward gripping pads, intended for test-fit on some 400 mL aerosol cans. The nominal outer diameter is 34.5 mm.

See [the source, measurements, and printing instructions](https://github.com/tipoftheaero/graffiti-tools/tree/main/tools/spray_can_cover_mini).

For generated STL and STEP assets, see [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/tipoftheaero/graffiti-tools/releases) once the release workflow has been run.

Do not force the cover onto a can or allow it to actuate the valve. Compatibility has not been established.
