# Printing guide

Default units are **millimeters**. Use the material and orientation suggestions in each tool's README.

- Check your slicer preview for missing thin walls or unsupported features.
- Verify scale: do not let the slicer interpret millimeters as inches.
- Measure critical fit dimensions with calipers before installing on cans.
- Use sufficient ventilation when working with aerosol paint and keep printed items away from heat/ignition.
- A passing CAD build does **not** certify structural integrity, suitability or fit.
